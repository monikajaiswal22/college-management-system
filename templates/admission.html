Eligibility Criteria
General Requirement: Minimum 50% marks in qualifying examination (45% for reserved categories)
For B.Tech:

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (minimum 60% marks) + Valid JEE Main / University Entrance Score

For MBA:

Bachelor's degree in any discipline (minimum 50% marks) + Valid CAT/MAT/CMAT/XAT Score

For MCA:

Bachelor's degree in BCA / B.Sc (IT/CS) with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level (minimum 50% marks)

For B.Com / BBA:

10+2 in any stream (minimum 50% marks) + Merit-based admission
